Ask your question
01Does OryvantAI replace our existing SIEM?+

No replacement is assumed. We assess the existing stack and native features first, then scope the integration or custom work that adds value.

02Do we need to replace our current security tools?+

No. The approach works around agreed customer systems, including SIEM, endpoint, identity and case management tools. Each integration depends on confirmed APIs, licences and permissions.

03Can AI automatically close security incidents?+

Read only and approval controlled workflows are the starting point. There is no silent case closure or unapproved production response. Any executable response requires separately validated permissions, change control and authority.

04How are company specific procedures handled?+

Approved policies, reviewed cases and named responsibilities form the knowledge base. AI can propose a structured SOP or playbook revision, but your procedure owner reviews and approves it before publication.

05Can we begin with one workflow?+

Yes. A bounded investigation, procedure or reporting workflow is a sensible starting point. The proposal defines the scope, reviewer, evidence and acceptance criteria.

06How is sensitive security data handled?+

The deployment plan defines approved data, processing location, access, retention, model use and provider terms. No cross client training is part of the default design. Permissions and customer approval are confirmed before connecting data.

07What types of organisations are a good fit?+

Teams with a recurring security workflow, an accountable owner and authorised access to usable evidence. Existing SOC teams, eligible managed providers and organisations with a defined improvement need can begin with a scoped review.
