BEFORE WE DEFINE THE SCOPE

Questions about
your environment.

01Are these existing OryvantAI customer sectors?+

These are relevant operating environments and example project scopes, not customer references or claims of completed deployments.

02Can we start with one workflow?+

Yes. Begin with a recurring task, permitted evidence, a named decision owner and an agreed operating boundary.

03Do you replace our existing security tools?+

No replacement is assumed. Discovery reviews existing tools and native capabilities before defining any custom work.

04Does every organisation have the same requirements?+

No. Requirements depend on the organisation, activity and jurisdiction. Applicability and processing arrangements must be confirmed for the specific scope.

05Can operational technology be included?+

An agreed scope can begin with authorised passive or read only telemetry. Active testing, production isolation and control system changes require the asset owner’s operational and safety approval.

06Can an existing managed provider work with OryvantAI?+

An eligible provider can explore an AI enablement scope around its existing team. Licensing where relevant, customer permissions, delivery boundaries and operating responsibilities must be confirmed first.
