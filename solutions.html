Ask your question
01Can we start with only one workflow?+

Yes. A bounded investigation, procedure or reporting workflow is a sensible starting point. The proposal defines the sources, reviewer, deliverables and acceptance criteria. Connect solution areas only where that improves a real operating task.

02Does OryvantAI replace our SIEM?+

No replacement is assumed. We assess your existing stack and native features first, then define the integration or custom work that adds value. Your SIEM remains an appropriate system for event storage, search and detection.

03Does AI make final security decisions?+

The analyst verifies context and owns the disposition. Read only and approval controlled workflows are the starting point. Any executable response requires separately validated permissions, change control and authorised approval.

04How does OryvantAI work with existing tools?+

Discovery confirms the systems, APIs, licences, approved evidence and access boundaries for your scope. Custom AI assistance connects permitted information and review workflows around your existing environment.

05Can our own SOPs and playbooks be used?+

Yes. Current approved policies, procedures, responsibilities and permitted example cases can form the knowledge base. Ownership, permissions and versions remain explicit.

06How are proposed procedure changes reviewed?+

A proposal explains what should change, the supporting cases or documents, open questions and the responsible owner. Your procedure owner reviews the draft. Consequential steps are tested before publication, with version history and a route back to the previous version.

07Can the solution support operational technology environments?+

An agreed scope can begin with authorised passive or read only telemetry. Operational changes, active testing or production isolation require the asset owner’s operational and safety approval. The permitted systems and delivery boundaries are confirmed first.

08How is evidence preserved?+

The workflow keeps source event references, original records and trusted calculations available for review. Briefs distinguish observed facts, inferences and missing information. Retention, access, export and handover arrangements are agreed for the deployment.
