BEFORE WE DEFINE THE SCOPE

Questions about
working together.

Begin with the workflow, the evidence and the people responsible for decisions.

01Do we need to replace our current security tools?+

No replacement is assumed. Discovery considers your current environment and native capabilities before defining custom work.

02Does OryvantAI replace our SOC team?+

The services support your team with evidence gathering, preparation and reviewable outputs. Analysts and authorised owners retain responsibility for decisions.

03Can OryvantAI work with our SIEM?+

The proposal confirms your platform, available APIs, licences, permissions and agreed integration scope. Support for every SIEM platform is not assumed.

04Can we start with only one service?+

Yes. Start with one recurring workflow, defined evidence, an accountable owner and acceptance criteria.

05Does AI automatically close incidents?+

No. There is no silent case closure or unapproved production response. Consequential actions require the appropriate human review and authorisation.

06Can OryvantAI use our internal procedures?+

Approved company procedures, roles and permitted example cases can ground the workflow. Knowledge ownership, access and versions remain explicit.

07How are service boundaries defined?+

Your proposal records supported systems, permitted evidence, deliverables, decision owners, review points, delivery permissions and acceptance criteria.

08Can services be combined later?+

Yes, where the connection improves a real operating task. Each extension requires an agreed scope, evidence foundation and clear responsibilities.
